BRAINSTORM: KI BEAM (right click) - Shulk's idea, not built yet
  Shulk's spec
    - Right click (instead of the disc) fires a Ki Beam: a long beam covering a large vertical stretch of the
      screen, about as wide as a small machine at base. Hits every machine it touches.
    - Unlocks with a Monolith clear, together with a new Ki system that powers it.
    - The player picks the look: Kamehameha, Galick Gun, Death Beam, Masenko, Special Beam Cannon, Final Flash...
      Same function, different visual (colour, core, charge glow, tip).
    - Its own upgrades: damage, length, width, split into 2/3/4/5 beams, burn chance (burn = damage over time).
    - Ki: a small pool at first (2-3 s of beaming). Upgrades: Ki cost per second, recharge speed, recharge amount,
      max Ki, and so on.
    - Works on the Monolith, but the disc and the beam can't fire at the same time.

  Questions to settle
    - Phones have no right click. Options: (a) a BEAM / DISC toggle button by the Techniques column; (b) hold with
      a second finger; (c) long-press switches. Desktop keeps right click. (a) is the clearest.
    - Aim: straight up from the finger (like the reference shot), toward the top of the screen, or along a drag?
      "Large vertical portion" suggests up from the touch point; dragging sideways sweeps it.
    - Does Ki reset on Zenkai (Shulk listed "ki amount" with things that reset), with the Ki upgrades permanent?
    - Unlock slot: the Monolith order is Companions, Workshop, Techniques, Item chests, SUPER chests, Workshop II,
      Store, Buu Wishes, Namek (10). The beam could open at Monolith 6 (alongside Workshop II) or 9.

  Ideas around it
    - Charge-up: holding before release builds a short charge (x1.5 damage), like a Kamehameha wind-up.
    - Splits fan out at a fixed angle (5 beams = a spread); each split beam does a share, so split trades focus
      for coverage. Good against swarms, worse on the Monolith (where only one beam can touch it).
    - Burn stacks up to N times on a machine; burning machines glow orange; a skill node "Scorched Earth"
      makes burn spread to neighbours on death.
    - Beam crits use the same tier system (CRIT, SUPER, ULTRA...), its own crit chance stat.
    - Beam clash on the Monolith: at the start of a fight, mash to win a short beam struggle for bonus damage.
    - Ki items: a Ki Drink (refill), "Kaio-ken Ki" buff (beam x2 but drains Ki twice as fast).
    - Companions with a matching beam (Vegeta + Galick Gun, Piccolo + Special Beam Cannon) get a small bonus while
      that beam is selected: a reason to pick a look beyond style.
    - Beam looks as unlockables: SUPER chest drops or Capsule Store cosmetics (cosmetic only, no power).
    - Upgrade homes: beam damage/length/width in the Disc tab (reset per run); split, burn and Ki pool in the
      Workshop (permanent); a skill tree branch for it.
    - Balance knobs for core.js: TUNE.beamKi (pool), kiRegen, kiCostPerSec, beamDmgShare (vs disc Ki Power),
      beamLen (game px), beamWidth, burnChance, burnDps, burnTime.

TODO: FIELD VARIETY (Shulk) - BUILT in v0.4
  The field should NEVER be all one machine type. When a machine spawns, first fill in any type from the floor's
  pool (core.js floorPool(f): up to 3 types) that isn't on the field yet; only once every type is present do the
  normal spawn weights (newest 20% / 35% / oldest 45%) pick duplicates.
  Where: core.js pickMachine(S) -> pass W in, check W.nodes for missing types from floorPool(S.floor), pick one of
  the missing ones (weighted among themselves), else the weighted roll. Caveats:
    - The very first floors only have 1-2 types in the pool, so "all one type" is unavoidable there (floor 1-2 =
      Scout Drones only). Fine, or let floors 1-2 borrow floor 3's second type.
    - Keeps farming fair: the rarest (newest) type always has at least one on the field, which speeds up parts for
      it. Re-run node tools/sim.js afterwards; rare parts get easier, so alloy gating may need a nudge.
    - Offline (core.js offline()) uses the pool weights directly; leave it, or blend toward an even split.
