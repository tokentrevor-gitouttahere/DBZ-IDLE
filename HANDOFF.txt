Browser-pane gotchas we hit (save yourself time):
- Screenshots sometimes come back stale or cropped/zoomed (DPR 1.5). Take a second screenshot; if it stays
  cropped, open a new tab (tabs_create + navigate) and close the old one. CSS zoom on <html> did not help.
- When the pane is hidden, requestAnimationFrame stalls: JS waits on rAF time out. Use setTimeout loops, or
  capture inside an rAF loop while a screenshot/wait keeps the pane visible.
- To show tiny pixel art, copy the field canvas / #faceCv into an overlay canvas at 4-6x (image-rendering:
  pixelated) and screenshot that. To stage a scene, edit DBZIdle.S (e.g. S.mono.tier, S.cards, S.helpers).
- Test saves: back up localStorage['dbzidle.v1'] first. To restore/erase without the game saving over you on
  unload: Storage.prototype.setItem = function(){}; then localStorage['dbzidle.v1'] = backup (or removeItem), then
  navigate. Bulk edits on Windows bash: heredocs with quotes broke; write helper scripts with the Write tool.
- Sim instrumentation: copy tools/sim.js to the scratchpad, point its require at src/core.js, inject hourly logs.

  Owner decisions: shares the forms' Ki pool; a HOLD toggle (DISC/BEAM) under FORM (key B; right click fires the
    beam whatever the toggle says); the beam starts at the HUD fighter, goes THROUGH the finger and on to the edge
    of the field (drag to sweep); unlock M9 (was a free slot); upgrades in a new KI BEAM tab of the DISC sheet, Zeni
    + alloy, reset by Zenkai (S.bup); "length" became Ki Efficiency; hits the Monolith and forms multiply it;
    beam crits use the disc's crit tiers; looks: Kamehameha (Goku) / Galick Gun (Vegeta) free and follow the fighter,

    (owner: "mechanics yet to be described"); extras: Ki Beam card (+5% beam damage), Ki Drink item (rare, refills
    Ki), Skill Tree BEAM branch with Scorched Earth (row 3 = M9: burns stack to 3).
  Numbers (TUNE): 0.5 x Ki Power per hit (incl. form/buffs), 8 hits/s, 7 Ki/s, needs 15 Ki to start, 10 px wide
    (+1.5/Width level; a machine is hit when its body is within half the width + 0.7r of the ray), split beams fan
    0.12 rad apart and hit for 60% each, Burn = 5%/level of beam dps for 3s after the last hit, ticking every 0.5s.
    Ki refills only in base form with the beam off. Out of Ki mid-hold: the disc takes over until you let go.
  Sim bot: full Ki bars alternate between the beam and the form; it buys beam upgrades like disc ones.
    24h x3: Monoliths 23/23/23 (baseline 24/24/24), floor 100 at 8h49-10h14 (baseline 9h14-10h06), M9 at 4h02-5h42.
    The bot spends less time transformed and spends Zeni/alloy on the beam, so it's ~1 Monolith behind by 24h.
  Saves: S.weapon, S.beamLook (kept), S.bup (reset); migrate() fills them. Cards now 151 (x_beam, i_kidrink).

v0.5 CARDS (Monolith 10, kept through Zenkai)                             core.js CARDS / cardRoll / transformCard
  What: 149 cards = 50 machine (+parts from it), 50 alloy (+double-refine chance), 6 Z-Fighter (+damage),
    6 form (-Ki drain in that form), 5 Technique (-cooldown), 15 item (+buff time; Senzu: chance to keep the bean),
    10 region (+PL XP there), 7 misc: Katchin (+Monolith damage; ALWAYS drops when you break a Monolith = the
    wiki's guaranteed Legendary Fish card), Loot Bulma (+her Zeni prize), Zenkai (+Zenkai Points), Capsule Chest
    (+chest meters), Bulma's Order (+Contract Points), Training Goal (+goal Zeni), Critical Hit (+crit damage).
  Owner decisions: owning a card = +5% to its own thing; NO bonus for the number of cards (they rejected it);
    gilding is called TRANSFORM: x3 effect (+15%), costs Rainbow Capsules, gold frame + glint (.ccard.gold).
  Drop rule (from the wiki): one roll per action with the chances ADDED (a kill dropping 12 parts rolls 12 x the
    per-part chance once). Offline: one roll per missing card with everything done while away added up, so at
    most one of each (200% still gives one). Live floor rolls are skipped offline (they add to the offline roll).
  Rates (tuned by sim): machine 1/(250 x 1.12^t) per part, alloy 1/(200 x 1.1^t) per alloy, Z-Fighter 1/20000
    per throw, form 1/900 per second, Technique 1/120 per use, item 1/25 per use, region 1/60 per floor, misc see
    code. First pass was far too generous (101/149 in 24h); now ~40-50 at 9h, ~80/149 (30-40 Transformed) at 24h,
    so the set is a long chase. Transform cost: machine/alloy 15 + 3t caps, others 40-120.
  UI: Menu > CARDS (kind tabs with counts; tile = art, name, perk, TRANSFORM button or how-to-get). Form and
    Z-Fighter card names follow the chosen fighter (cardName in ui.js). NEW CARD banner; cards in the away report.
  Note: in the sim the Technique cards come slowly because the bot rarely sees 3+ machines (Z-Fighters clear the
    field); a real player fires Techniques plenty.

v0.5 Z POINTS + Z-FIGHTERS (companions renamed everywhere players see it)    core.js zpZeni / zpCost / zpMax / buyZp
  Owner spec: Zeni buys Z points; Z points hire and upgrade Z-Fighters (like Shminer's drone points); Z points
    and the price progress survive Zenkai; dumping Zeni into Z points before a Zenkai is the intended strategy.
  Curve (owner's numbers): the first Zeni buys 10 points (= Yamcha's first Damage level), then 1 Zeni each up to
    750 bought, then point n costs (n/750)^1.824 Zeni, so the millionth costs ~501K (TUNE.zpFirst/zpFlat/zpPow).
  Owner decisions: hiring costs Z points too; keep the old upgrade caps; dumping is a HINT only (no buy button in
    the Zenkai window); hints in a loading splash AND the Zenkai window.
  Pricing reasoning: total Zeni for N points grows ~N^2.82, so old Zeni costs map to Z costs ~^0.354. Hire Z:
    Yamcha 20, Krillin 26, Gohan 60, Piccolo 150, Vegeta 380, Nappa 940; upgrade = hire x mult x g^level with g
    = old g^0.354 (dmg 1.103, spd 1.154, multi 2.41, crit 1.082, trait 1.112); Multishot mult cut 25 -> 8 so it
    stays worth buying. Measured: levels track the old Zeni system (a few levels ahead), 1M points ~hour 11-12.
  UI: Capsule Corp > Z-FIGHTERS: exchange bar (1x/25x/250x/2500x/MAX, cost -> +points, next point price),
    balance with the .zpico orange-ball icon. #splash: random tip (40% the Zenkai-dump tip), >= 2.2s, tap to skip.

v0.4 VEGETA'S TECHNIQUE (owner)
  Quick barrage of weak blasts: every 0.6s at 0.05x power per blast; 2 blasts at Trait 0, doubling per Trait level
  up to 64 (Trait 5); later Trait levels add +10% blast damage each (owner chose "double, then +damage" because
  doubling 40 levels is impossible). Keeps a fixed Saiyan Pride (+50% parts) on hits (owner: keep both).

v0.4 NO ARMOR ON PLANET 1 (owner)
  machArmor = 0 for machine types 0-24; only the Monolith has armor until Planet 2 (types 25+, formula unchanged).
  Reasoning recorded: we measured first; with armor the disc lost <= 1% of its damage on every Planet 1 type, and
  sims of old / no armor / HP x1.15 / x1.3 / x1.45 were all within noise. Chose Planet 1 HP x1.15 to cover first
  visits to a new type, where armor used to slow you. Krillin's armor pierce now matters only on the Monolith and
  Planet 2.

v0.4 FIELD VARIETY + Z-FIGHTER PROJECTILES
  pickMachine(S, W): any floor-pool type missing from the field spawns first (floors 1-2 only have one type).
  Each Z-Fighter throws its own pixel-drawn projectile (render.js shotPos/drawShot): Yamcha weaving Spirit Ball,
  Krillin spinning Kienzan, Gohan Masenko, Piccolo Special Beam Cannon with spiral, Vegeta purple barrage (Goku in
  the slot: blue), Nappa big Break Cannon orb.
